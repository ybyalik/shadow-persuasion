# Shadow Persuasion

## How to Stop Being Used and Take Your Power Back

*Nate Harlan*

---

**A note before you begin**

This book is about everyday influence: how people pressure each other, and how to respond calmly and fairly. It is for general information and self-help only. It is not therapy, medical advice, financial advice, or legal advice, and it is not a substitute for professional help.

If you are in a relationship where you feel afraid, have been physically hurt, or are being threatened, please reach out for support. In the United States, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is available at any hour at 1-800-799-7233, or by texting START to 88788. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.

The examples in this book are composites, written to illustrate common situations. Any resemblance to specific people is coincidental.
