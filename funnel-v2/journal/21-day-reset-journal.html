How to use this journal

This journal goes with Part Two of Shadow Persuasion. Each day takes about fifteen minutes: a short note in the morning, the drill when the moment comes, and a few questions in the evening.

Write by hand if you can. Writing is slower than thinking, and slower is the point.

The five rules. Low stakes first. No big confrontations. Missed days are fine, just pick up where you left off. Write something every evening, even two lines. Expect pushback, and don't treat it as failure.

A word on safety. If someone in your life is abusive or threatening, don't use these drills on that person. Practice with everyone else, and reach out for support. In the US, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.

Quick Start, Day 1

The pause

Today's drill. Don't agree to anything on the spot. Use "Let me check and get back to you." Done

Morning

Name one person you expect to ask you for something today.

Evening

How many requests did you get today?

How many times did you pause? How many times did the instant yes slip out?

Once you had time to think, did any requests look different?

How did people react to "let me check"? Write what actually happened.
